🔥 Deep Self-Discovery Prompts (Open-Ended Version)
💭 1. What do you believe is your purpose in life right now?
It can be simple, big, messy, evolving—just your honest truth.

😨 2. What’s one thing you’re genuinely afraid of when it comes to your future?
Something that lingers in the back of your mind or keeps you up sometimes.

🌱 3. Out of these 8 areas of life, which 3 are most important to you right now?
(Physical, Intellectual, Occupational, Spiritual, Environmental, Financial, Social, Emotional)
Why do they matter to you at this stage in your life?

🔁 4. What’s something you already do—daily or weekly—that reflects your commitment to those 3 areas?
Think of small habits, routines, or rituals that keep you grounded.

“Need inspiration?” examples like:
"I try to walk 10,000 steps a day because physical health helps clear my mind."
"I journal about what I’m grateful for every night to stay spiritually connected."
"I’m studying 1 hour a day to prep for the job I want next year."
